About

Hi, I'm Jai.

I teach the way I learned: clean technique, real musicality, and a lot of fun along the way.

I've been dancing for years, training in hip hop, choreography and freestyle, and I love sharing that with anyone who wants to move.

Whether it's your first class or you've been dancing your whole life, you'll leave with a new routine and a little more confidence.
